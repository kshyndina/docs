## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up at [customers.triton.one/onboarding](https://customers.triton.one/onboarding) or contact [support@triton.one](mailto:support@triton.one) for custom plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your endpoint and token">
    After signing up, access your **Endpoints** and **Tokens** from the [Customer Portal](https://customers.triton.one). Use your endpoint URL for frontend dApps and your secret token for backend services.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first call">
    Point your Solana SDK at your endpoint URL (`https://your-app.mainnet.rpcpool.com`) and start making JSON-RPC calls immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore advanced features">
    Unlock streaming with [Dragon's Mouth gRPC](/streaming/dragons-mouth-grpc), MEV protection with [Yellowstone Shield](/solana/shield-policies), or swap routing with the [Metis API](/trading-apis/metis-swap).
  </Step>
</Steps>
