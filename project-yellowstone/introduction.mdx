Solana RPC 2.0

The Yellowstone projects form a service explicitly built for high-performance bchain data delivery. Backend services can use gRPC subscriptions through Dragon's Mouth, and front-end GUIs will feel faster due to Steamboat custom indexes and Whirligig WebSockets.

Together with the Solana Foundation, we have also built a new historical archive called Old Faithful, through which all of Solana's transaction and b data is available at low cost and through decentralised means.
