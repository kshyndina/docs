Triton One is a high-performance blockchain RPC infrastructure platform designed for teams that need reliable, low-latency access to on-chain data. Whether you're building a consumer dApp, running a high-frequency trading operation, streaming live chain data, or operating a validator, Triton provides the infrastructure layer to support your work at any scale.

## Supported chains

Triton provides dedicated, reliable RPC access across multiple high-performance blockchains:

- **Solana** — Mainnet, testnet, and devnet, with the full suite of Triton's advanced features
- **Sui** — Mainnet RPC including Walrus decentralized storage support
- **Monad** — High-performance RPC for Monad's EVM-compatible chain
- **Pythnet** — Dedicated nodes for the Pyth Network's price oracle chain
