## About Triton

What Triton is, what we build, and how Project Yellowstone fits into the stack.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Triton One?">
    Triton is a high-performance infrastructure provider running RPC nodes, private validators, and data streaming systems across Solana, Sui, Monad, and other networks.

    It delivers reliable, fast, and feature-rich blockchain infrastructure trusted by developers, validators, and traders that can't afford downtime.

## Getting started

How to set up an account, decide on a plan, and add users to your customer dashboard.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can I get started with Triton services? / How do I get an account?">
    For custom inquiries, contact support by clicking the chat icon in the bottom right of your [customer dashboard](https://customers.triton.one), or reach us on [Telegram](https://t.me/tritonone).

  <Accordion title="How can I decide which service fits my traffic needs best?">
    We offer personalised consultations to review your traffic patterns and recommend the right setup. Please reach out via our [inquiry page](https://triton.one/contact).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I add a user to my Triton customer dashboard?">
    We're working on self-service user management, but in the meantime, reach out through the support channel on your customer dashboard, and we'll add them for you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you require long-term contracts?">
    No, our standard billing is month-to-month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my plan later?">
    Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan as your needs change.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Triton offer a free trial to test its services?">
    Triton does not offer testing of dedicated nodes because they run on custom bare-metal servers that require significant upfront costs, long provisioning times, and multi-month vendor commitments. For now, we provide trials only on our shared infrastructure so you can evaluate reliability and latency. [Contact us here](https://triton.one/contact) to set it up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I run custom software on Triton's nodes?">
    No. We offer managed services; therefore, no one outside the Triton team has access to the infrastructure. We don't provide SSH access to our infrastructure. If you have dedicated nodes, we can set up the supported add-ons you request.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Products and services

Questions about Triton's services beyond Solana RPC.

<Accordion title="What Pyth oracle products do you offer?">
  We provide infrastructure for PythNet RPC and Hermes for streaming price updates. We don't offer benchmarks. For publishers, we offer dedicated RPC to publish onto PythNet and Validators to bolster the Pyth Network.

  We work closely with Douro Labs (Pyth Team) to ensure a smooth onboarding and service experience.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How can I access Pyth Hermes?">
  To access Hermes, you need to point your endpoint to access Hermes and Pythnet. Make sure your path looks like:

  ```text
  https://<endpoint>.mainnet.pythnet.rpcpool.com/<secret token>/hermes/v2/<rest of the call>
  ```

  The most common mistake is omitting the token, `v2`, or `hermes` parts of the path.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Triton have a validator?">
  Yes. You can stake directly with our Private Trusted Validator to earn weekly USDC rewards.
</Accordion>
