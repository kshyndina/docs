## Sign-in

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I sign in with Google?">
    Google single sign-on is reserved for Triton One employees and isn't available on customer accounts. Sign in with your Triton username and password instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why won't my email work to sign in?">
    The login form takes your username, not your email address. If you've forgotten your username, click **Forgot your username** at the bottom of the form, enter your email, and we'll send it to you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing and usage

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I top up my balance?">
    Open your customer dashboard, go to **Billing**, and click **Buy credits**. You can pay in stablecoins via:

    - **Wallet Connect.** Connect your wallet and pay with any supported token.
    - **Transfer from anywhere.** Send stablecoins from any external wallet or exchange.

    If you'd rather pay by card or wire, switch to invoiced billing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I set up low-balance notifications?">
    Yes. Open your customer dashboard, go to **Billing**, toggle alerts on, and set a threshold (for example, $10). When your balance hits that amount, every user on the account gets a notification.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I see my usage?">
    In your customer dashboard, open the **v3 Billing** tab. You'll see total requests and GB used (the two dimensions you're billed on), plus a per-service breakdown table below with product-level detail. To export it, click **Export CSV** in the top-right corner.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my gRPC bill spike all at once?">
    gRPC streaming is metered when the connection closes. If you hold a stream open for several hours or a day, the full bandwidth charge is applied to your account once it disconnects.

    Also, usage data refreshes every \~4 hours, so you can expect a lag between actual traffic and what's reflected in the dashboard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Invoicing and payments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When will I receive my invoice, and what period does it cover?">
    We send two kinds of invoices:

    - **Node or shared subscription invoices** are sent on the first day of each month as prepayment for the upcoming month's services.
    - **Add-on service invoices** (e.g., Cascade, Metaplex DAS) are metered and billed monthly. They're sent early in the following month, covering the previous month's usage.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a due date for paying the invoice?">
    Yes, all invoices are due within 7 days of the issue date.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you support?">
    We accept:

    - USDC (SPL, ERC20 on Ethereum, Tron, Polygon)
    - Payments through hel.io (USDC)
    - Credit cards (via Coinflow)
    - Wire transfers in GBP and USD

    Credit card payments are available only for customers using shared services. For any billing or account-related questions, please [contact us](https://triton.one/contact).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    For dedicated nodes and invoiced clients, we require one calendar month's notice. This aligns with the notice periods from our own infrastructure vendors.

    For PAYG customers, there's nothing to cancel: if you don't top up, you don't pay anything. Any unused balance remains valid for one year from the date of the deposit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If you're already a customer, you can reach us directly through the support channel in your [customer dashboard](https://customers.triton.one).
