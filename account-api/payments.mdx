Triton One offers flexible payment options to suit both crypto-native teams and traditional businesses. Billing is month-to-month with no long-term commitment required. Your account stays active as long as your payment stream or balance is current. For billing questions, contact [support@triton.one](mailto:support@triton.one).

## Accepted payment methods

Triton One accepts the following payment methods:

| Method | Details |
|---|---|
| **USDC (SPL)** | Solana-native USDC via hel.io pay stream |
| **USDC (ERC-20, Ethereum)** | Ethereum USDC via hel.io |
| **USDC (Tron)** | Tron USDC via hel.io |
| **USDC (Polygon)** | Polygon USDC via hel.io |
| **Credit card** | Processed via Coinflow |
| **Wire transfer** | Available in GBP or USD — contact support to arrange |

<Note>
  Wire transfer payments must be arranged directly with Triton. Email [support@triton.one](mailto:support@triton.one) to set up a wire billing relationship.
</Note>

## Billing cycle

Triton One bills month-to-month. There are no annual contracts or lock-in periods. Your subscription remains active for as long as payments are current.

## Activating your account with hel.io

The most common activation flow uses hel.io's web3 pay stream. Once you set up a pay stream, your account activates automatically and stays active as long as the stream is running.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to the Customer Portal">
    Go to [customers.triton.one](https://customers.triton.one) and sign in to your Triton One account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the inactive account banner">
    If your account is inactive, you'll see a prompt on the dashboard. Click on the account name or the "here" link in the banner to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the pay stream page">
    From the account dashboard, click the **Inactive Pay Stream** banner to open the hel.io pay stream setup page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Click **Connect Wallet** on the hel.io page. Select **Web** or **Extension** depending on your wallet type, then click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the connection">
    Your wallet will prompt you to approve the connection. Click **Approve**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the payment settings">
    Review the duration and price for the pay stream. When the details look correct, click **Pay**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the transaction">
    The page shows "Approving transaction" while the blockchain confirms your payment. Once complete, you'll receive a confirmation email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify account activation">
    Return to [customers.triton.one](https://customers.triton.one) and refresh the page. Your account should now show as active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Stopping a pay stream

To stop recurring payments, click **Stop Pay Stream** on your account dashboard in the hel.io pay stream section, then approve the transaction in your wallet. Your account will remain active through the end of the current billing period.

## Cancellation policy

Cancelling your Triton One service requires **one calendar month's notice**. To initiate cancellation, contact [support@triton.one](mailto:support@triton.one) with your account details and intended cancellation date.

<Warning>
  Cancellations take effect at the end of the one-month notice period, not immediately. Payments due during the notice period are still owed.
</Warning>

## Billing questions

For all billing-related inquiries — including wire transfer setup, invoice requests, or questions about your current balance — contact [support@triton.one](mailto:support@triton.one).
