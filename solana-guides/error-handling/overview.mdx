When something doesn't work, jump to the relevant category below. Each page lists the symptom, the cause, and the fix.

## Common first-call issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="401 Unauthorized" icon="lock">
    The token is wrong, or you're calling a public endpoint from an origin not on its allowlist. Re-check the token in the portal under your endpoint's **Auth** tab, or add your origin (e.g. `https://yourapp.com`) to the allowlist. Full details: [RPC errors](/solana-guides/error-handling/rpc).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="429 Too Many Requests" icon="gauge">
    You've hit the rate limit on the endpoint or the calling IP. Pause for 10 seconds, then resume. See [Rate and connection limits](/solana/get-started/rate-and-connection-limits) for the full numbers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Connection timeout from a browser" icon="globe">
    Public endpoints require an origin allowlist. Open **Auth** in the portal, add the origin you're calling from, save, and retry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="403 on gRPC" icon="shield-x">
    Token must be passed as `x-token` metadata, not in the URL path. See [Streaming errors](/solana-guides/error-handling/streaming) for the gRPC auth pattern.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
