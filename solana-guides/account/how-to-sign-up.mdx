<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer portal" titleSize="h3">
    Go to [customers.triton.one/users/sign-up](https://customers.triton.one/users/sign-up). Sign up with email, GitHub, or Google.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email" titleSize="h3">
    Click the link in the verification email. The portal will open to your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top up the $125 minimum" titleSize="h3">
    Open Billing, then Buy credits. Pay in stablecoins (USDC, USDT, DAI) via Wallet Connect or any external wallet/exchange. The deposit is prepaid, non-refundable, and valid for 12 months. There is no free trial -- the deposit replaces it.

    For card or wire payment, switch to [invoiced billing](/solana/get-started/plans-and-billing) -- contact support by clicking the chat icon in the bottom right of your customer dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first endpoint" titleSize="h3">
    Click **Create endpoint**, pick **Solana mainnet** (or devnet for testing), and choose a region. The portal returns:

    - **Endpoint URL** -- `<your-endpoint>.mainnet.rpcpool.com`
    - **Secret token** -- a long random string

    Keep the token server-side. For browser apps, set up an [origin allowlist](/solana/get-started/auth-and-security) instead of using the token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first request" titleSize="h3">
    Follow the [Quickstart](/solana/get-started/quickstart) to call `getSlot` and confirm the endpoint is live. About five minutes end to end.
  </Step>
</Steps>
