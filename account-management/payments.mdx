---
title: "Payments"
description: "Here is a step-by-step guide on how to sign up and process payments on Hel.io via the Triton One customer site:"
---

1. Open your preferred web browser and go to https://customers.triton.one and login to your Triton One account.

<figure><img src="/images/Screenshot%202023-03-21%20at%2010.28.47%20PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. You will be prompted that your RPC account is still inactive. To activate the account, make a payment via Hel.io web3 payment. You can opt to click on the account name (follow step #3) or click on "here" or the banner on the link (directly proceeds to step #4).

<figure><img src="/images/Quel%20est%20le%20mail%20du%20service%20client%20.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. You will be routed to the account dashboard, which shows the account is still inactive. Click on the "Inactive Pay Stream" banner.

<figure><img src="/images/Screenshot%202023-03-21%20at%2010.30.14%20PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. You see the Hel.io Pay Stream page. Click on "Connect Wallet”.

<figure><img src="/images/Screenshot%202023-03-21%20at%2010.31.16%20PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Select "Web" or "Extension" if prompted.

<figure><img src="/images/Screenshot%202023-03-21%20at%2010.30.35%20PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Tap on "Connect".

<figure><img src="/images/Screenshot%202023-03-21%20at%2010.30.57%20PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Click "Approve".

<figure><img src="/images/Screenshot%202023-03-21%20at%2010.32.04%20PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8. Check the settings for duration and price. If all entries are correct click "Pay".

<figure><img src="/images/Screenshot%202023-03-23%20at%208.51.41%20PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

9. The page will show "Approving transaction".

<figure><img src="/images/Screenshot%202023-03-21%20at%2010.33.29%20PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

10. &#x20;Payment is done! To stop the pay stream, click on "Stop Pay Stream".

<figure><img src="/images/Screenshot%202023-03-21%20at%2010.37.19%20PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

11. Click "Approve".

<figure><img src="/images/Screenshot%202023-03-21%20at%2010.37.56%20PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

12. Payment confirmation is sent via email.

<figure><img src="/images/Image%203-24-23%20at%207.05%20PM.jpeg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

13. Navigate back to https://customers.triton.one. You may need to refresh the page to display the activated RPC account.

<figure><img src="/images/Screenshot%202023-03-24%20at%2012.40.38%20PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
