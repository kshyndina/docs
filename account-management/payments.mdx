Here is a step-by-step guide on how to sign up and process payments on Hel.io via the Triton One customer site:

1. Open your preferred web browser and go to https://customers.triton.one and login to your Triton One account.

!

1. You will be prompted that your RPC account is still inactive. To activate the account, make a payment via Hel.io web3 payment. You can opt to click on the account name (follow step #3) or click on "here" or the banner on the link (directly proceeds to step #4).

!

1. You will be routed to the account dashboard, which shows the account is still inactive. Click on the "Inactive Pay Stream" banner.

!

1. You see the Hel.io Pay Stream page. Click on "Connect Wallet”.

!

1. Select "Web" or "Extension" if prompted.

!

1. Tap on "Connect".

!

1. Click "Approve".

!

1. the settings for duration and price. If all entries are correct click "Pay".

!

1. The page will show "Approving transaction".

!

1. Payment is done! To stop the pay stream, click on "Stop Pay Stream".

!

1. Click "Approve".

!

1. Payment confirmation is sent via email.

!

1. Navigate back to https://customers.triton.one. You may need to refresh the page to display the activated RPC account.

!
