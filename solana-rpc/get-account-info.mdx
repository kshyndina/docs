---
title: "getAccountInfo"
description: "Returns full information about an account, including its data, owner program, lamports, and rent status."
api: 'POST https://api.mainnet-beta.solana.com'
authMethod: "none"
---

Returns full information about an account: its lamport balance, owner program, executable flag, rent epoch, and the raw data the account holds. Use this to inspect any account on Solana -- system accounts, token accounts, program-derived accounts, etc.

## Request body

<ParamField body="jsonrpc" type="string" required>
  Always `"2.0"`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="id" type="integer" required>
  An identifier you choose.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="method" type="string" required>
  Always `"getAccountInfo"`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="params" type="array" required>
  `[pubkey, config?]`:
  - `pubkey` (string, required) -- the base58 account address
  - `config.encoding` (string, optional) -- one of `"base58"`, `"base64"`, `"base64+zstd"`, or `"jsonParsed"`. Defaults to `"base58"`.
  - `config.commitment` (string, optional) -- `"processed"`, `"confirmed"`, or `"finalized"`.
  - `config.dataSlice` (object, optional) -- request only a slice of the data: `{ offset: int, length: int }`.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="result.context.slot" type="integer">
  The slot the data was retrieved at.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="result.value" type="object">
  The account, or `null` if the account does not exist.
  - `lamports` (integer) -- balance in lamports
  - `owner` (string) -- base58 address of the program that owns the account
  - `data` (string or array) -- the account data, encoded as requested
  - `executable` (boolean) -- whether the account contains an executable program
  - `rentEpoch` (integer) -- the epoch when the account next owes rent
  - `space` (integer) -- the size of the account data in bytes
</ResponseField>
