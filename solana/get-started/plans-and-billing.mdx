Triton has **two billing methods** and **two infrastructure types**. Pick one of each and you have your setup.

| | PAYG | Invoiced |
|---|---|---|
| **Shared infrastructure** | Self-onboard, pay as you go. Default for most teams. | Same shared pool, billed monthly via invoice. |
| **Dedicated infrastructure** | Available on request. | Most dedicated customers are here, monthly invoice, custom sizing. |

Either axis can combine. PAYG is the fastest start; invoicing is the standard for procurement-driven companies. Shared is fine for the vast majority of workloads; dedicated is for trading systems, very high RPS, or custom Geyser configurations.

### How to use the calculator

- **Custom PAYG** — drag each service slider to the volume you expect for the month. The total is what you'd burn through at that rate. Sliders are estimates only; actual usage drives actual draw-down. Minimum deposit is `$125`.
- **Dedicated nodes** — pick a chain. Pricing is fixed-per-month for the node plus included streaming. Other services drawdown at the same per-call rate as PAYG.

## Getting started: $125 prepaid deposit

A `$125` prepaid, non-refundable deposit kicks off your account. The deposit is **valid for up to one year** and burns down as you use services. Top up at any time; unused balance carries over until the year is up.

There is no free trial. The deposit replaces it; one `$125` deposit gives you a year of testing room with the same account, endpoint, and tokens you'd ship to production.

## Invoicing

Same pricing, but billed monthly with a **`$125` minimum invoice** instead of prepaid drawdown. Use this when:

- You need a procurement-friendly contract (NET-30, NET-60).
- Finance prefers monthly invoices over auto-deductions.
- You're a high-volume customer where prepaid would mean managing very large balances.

Sign up via the same flow and choose **Invoiced** when prompted, or click the chat icon in your [customer dashboard](https://customers.triton.one) to switch over.

## Dedicated infrastructure

A dedicated stack means your own validator(s), edge router(s), and gRPC streamers, sized to your workload. Most dedicated customers are on monthly invoicing, but PAYG dedicated is available on request.

When to consider dedicated:

- **Trading systems** with strict latency budgets (`<50ms`) to a specific region.
- **Heavy `getProgramAccounts`** workloads that would saturate the shared pool.
- **High-cardinality streaming** with custom Geyser plugins or filter logic.
- **Custom rate limits** beyond what the shared pool offers.

Sizing happens with your account manager. [Contact sales](https://triton.one/contact) to start that conversation.

## Cancellation

| | PAYG (shared) | PAYG (dedicated) | Invoiced |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Notice period** | None. Credits expire after 1 year if unused, otherwise burn down | 1 calendar month notice | 1 calendar month notice |
| **Cancel from** | Portal or support | Support | Support |

PAYG on shared is the lightest commitment: stop using the service and the credits eventually expire. Dedicated and invoiced both require a calendar-month notice so we can wind the infrastructure down cleanly.

## Payment methods

Card via Coinflow, USDC (SPL on Solana, ERC-20 on Ethereum), hel.io, and wire (USD / GBP) for invoiced customers. See [How to top up credits](/solana/get-started/account-management/how-to-top-up-credits) for the per-method walkthrough.
