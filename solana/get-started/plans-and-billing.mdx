Triton has three ways to pay: **PAYG** (default), **committed** (pre-paid credit pool, discounted), and **dedicated** (your own node). Pick PAYG to start; switch later without re-provisioning.

## Compare plans

| | PAYG | Committed | Dedicated |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | Trying things out, dev environments, low-volume production | Steady production traffic | Latency-sensitive trading, isolated capacity, custom rate limits |
| **Billing** | Per-request + per-CU on shared pool | Pre-pay credits monthly at a discount | Hourly per node + add-ons |
| **Rate limit** | Default 1,200 req / 10s per IP | Higher per-IP budget | Custom (negotiated) |
| **Streaming connections** | Limited per plan | Higher per plan | Custom |
| **Region pinning** | Closest available, automatic | Same | You pick |
| **Setup time** | Instant | Instant | A few hours (provisioning) |

## How PAYG works

Pay only for what you use. Two meters:

1. **Per request** -- a base price per RPC call, with multipliers for expensive methods (`getProgramAccounts`, `getTokenAccountsByOwner`, etc.).
2. **Per compute unit** -- some methods scale with the data they process. The portal's billing dashboard shows the cost split.

Add-on pricing for specialised products:

| Product | Price |
|---|---|
| **DAS API** (NFTs, compressed assets) | $50 per million requests |
| **Hydrant Archive** (historical queries) | $10 per million queries; minimum $10/month if used |

## How committed works

Pre-pay a credit pool monthly at a per-credit discount over PAYG. Credits roll within the month. Good fit when:

- Your traffic is predictable.
- You're doing more than $200--300/month on PAYG (the break-even depends on method mix).
- You want a higher per-IP rate-limit budget without going to a dedicated node.

Switching is one click in the portal. PAYG fallback kicks in if you exhaust your monthly credits.

## How dedicated works

Your own validator(s) and edge router(s), rented hourly. Use cases:

- **Trading systems** that need `<50ms` latency to a specific region and zero noisy-neighbour effects.
- **Heavy `getProgramAccounts`** workloads that would saturate shared pools.
- **Custom configurations** -- specific Geyser plugins, larger gRPC fan-out, custom filters.

Speak to support for sizing. We'll size based on:

- Expected RPS and method mix.
- Streaming subscription count and filter complexity.
- Region requirements.
- Add-ons: SWQoS, Steamboat, Hydrant.

## Payment methods

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Credit / debit card** | via Coinflow. Auto-renewal supported. |
| **USDC** | SPL on Solana, ERC-20 on Ethereum. |
| **hel.io** | One-click payment widget. |
| **Wire transfer** | GBP and USD. |

For payment-method changes or invoice queries, open the **Billing** tab in the portal or [reach out to support](/solana/get-started/account-management/reach-out-to-support).

## How to top up credits

Step-by-step in [How to top up credits](/solana/get-started/account-management/how-to-top-up-credits). Auto-top-up is recommended for production accounts.

## Cancellation and notice

Month-to-month billing on PAYG and committed. Dedicated nodes require **one calendar month notice** to cancel. Cancel from the portal or via support.

## When to upgrade

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I'm hitting 429s during traffic spikes" icon="gauge">
    First, check whether you're hitting the **general** budget or a **method-specific** one (the response headers tell you). If it's general, a higher PAYG / committed tier raises the per-IP budget. If it's method-specific (e.g. `getProgramAccounts`), move that workload to [Steamboat](/solana/reading-state/steamboat-indexed-accounts) or a dedicated node.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My latency is inconsistent under load" icon="zap">
    Shared endpoints have noisy-neighbour effects. A dedicated node in your closest region eliminates this. Trading systems should usually be dedicated from day one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I need a Geyser plugin or gRPC config that isn't on shared" icon="puzzle">
    Dedicated only. Talk to support; we'll provision a node with your config baked in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My DAS / Hydrant usage is over $500/month" icon="archive">
    Talk to support about a committed deal -- we typically discount when usage is steady.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
