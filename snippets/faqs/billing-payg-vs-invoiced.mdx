<Accordion title="What's the difference between PAYG and invoiced billing?">
  PAYG draws from a prepaid balance you top up; invoiced bills you at the start of each month for the month. PAYG is fastest to start (sign up at [customers.triton.one](https://customers.triton.one/users/sign-up)). Invoiced is what most teams switch to once usage stabilises -- bills are predictable and overages are billed at the same rate instead of cutting service. See [Plans and billing](/solana/get-started/plans-and-billing) for the full comparison.
</Accordion>
