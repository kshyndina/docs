<Accordion title="How is streaming priced?">
  All streaming services (Dragon's Mouth, Whirligig, Fumarole, Old Faithful, Hermes) are billed at $0.08 per GB of bandwidth sent. You only pay for data your client actually consumes -- there's no per-call or per-subscription fee. See [streaming best practices](/solana/streaming/best-practices) for filtering tips that reduce bandwidth.
</Accordion>
