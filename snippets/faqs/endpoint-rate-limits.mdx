<Accordion title="What rate limits apply to my endpoint?">
  Rate limits depend on your plan and the specific RPC method you're calling -- some methods (like `getProgramAccounts`) are stricter than others. If you hit a limit you'll see HTTP 429 on REST or RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED on gRPC. See [Rate and connection limits](/solana/get-started/rate-and-connection-limits) for the per-method tables.
</Accordion>
